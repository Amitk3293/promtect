Anyone may read, run, and (within the license) redistribute the code. The name is
the part you cannot copy. This policy means a fork stays a fork: it can use the
code, but it cannot present itself as "Promtect" and trade on the trust of the
project. That is the floor that protects users from confusing or malicious rebuilds.

Promtect"), not Promtect itself. Do not reuse the logo.
