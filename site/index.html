Three steps, zero changes to how you work

01

Mask outbound

72 detectors find secrets in the request and replace each with an opaque sentinel before it leaves your machine.

02

Forward clean

Promtect re-originates HTTPS to Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, or a local model. The auth header passes through untouched.

03

Restore inbound

As the answer streams back token-by-token, sentinels become your real values — or stay masked, in strict mode. Your call.
